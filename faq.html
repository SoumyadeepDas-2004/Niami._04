FAQ

Have questions about commissioning me? Here are some answers to help you understand the commission process.

General & Ordering

How do I place a commission order?

To place an order, contact me through Instagram and describe what you'd like me to draw. Include your character references and any specific requirements.

I'll discuss the details, availability and price with you before confirming the commission.

Can I commission artwork of multiple characters?

Yes! You can request artwork featuring multiple characters. Please mention how many characters you want and provide references for each one.

The final price will depend on the number of characters and the complexity of the artwork.

Are there additional charges for extra characters or complex designs?

Additional charges may apply depending on the number of characters, design complexity and extra details requested.

I'll confirm the final price with you before starting the artwork.

References & Artwork

What references should I provide?

Please provide clear character references, including their appearance, hairstyle, clothing, colours and important details.

You can also share pose references, expressions or visual examples to help explain your idea.

Can I commission artwork without a reference image?

Yes! You can describe your character or idea in writing. Include as many details as possible, such as appearance, clothing, colours and pose.

I'll let you know if I need any additional information before starting.

Process & Revisions

How long does a commission take?

Completion time depends on the complexity of the artwork and my current workload.

I'll provide an estimated delivery time when we discuss your commission.

Will I see the sketch before the final artwork?

I'll discuss the sketch preview and approval process with you when confirming your commission.

Can I request changes during the sketch stage?

Yes, you can communicate your requested adjustments during the sketch stage. Please explain the changes clearly so I can understand what you'd like modified.

Can I request changes after the artwork is completed?

Major changes after completion may not be possible. Please review the sketch and provide your feedback during the approval stage.

Any changes requested after completion will be discussed separately.

Can I cancel my commission after work has started?

If you need to cancel, please contact me as soon as possible. Cancellation and refund eligibility will depend on the stage of work and my commission terms.

Delivery & Usage

How will I receive my finished artwork?

Your completed artwork will be delivered digitally through an agreed delivery method.

I'll share the finished file with you once the commission is complete.

Can I post the commissioned artwork on social media?

Personal social media sharing is welcome. Please credit me as the artist when posting your commissioned artwork.

Commercial use, resale and redistribution are subject to the agreed usage terms.

Refunds & Contact

Are commissions refundable?

Refund eligibility depends on the progress of the commission and the terms agreed upon before the order was confirmed.

Please contact me if you have questions about a refund.

How can I contact you about a commission?

You can contact me through Instagram for commission enquiries, questions or updates.

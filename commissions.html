Commission Information

Terms of Service 📖

  • For personal use only. You may post the artwork on social media with credit.
  • Do not use my artwork or claim it as your own.
  • Strictly DO NOT feed my artwork to AI.
  • Please provide clear references for your character, such as reference sheets, poses and character descriptions.
  • Full payment upfront. I will start your commission after receiving the full payment.
  • No refunds once you have approved the sketch.
  • I have the right to decline commissions that make me uncomfortable or are beyond my capabilities.
  • I will update you on the commission progress. Feel free to request changes (maximum 2 revisions).
  • No changes can be made once I start colouring.
  • Your commission will take approximately 5 days to 2 weeks to complete after I start working on it.
  • Your finished commission will be sent via email.
  • Payment methods: TNG e-wallet, local QR payment or online banking.
